## 1. The version built

**ZIL-130 flatbed with drop sides, the 1977+ ZIL-130-76/-80 look** (owner, 2026-10-28: every
truck an open flatbed): the V8 petrol, five-speed, 4×2 with dual rear wheels. The roads of
around 2000 saw this one most; the cab, bonnet and front are the same 1962–94 stamping, the
grille and lamps changed with the facelifts (question F1 below).

## 4. Feature inventory (for the owner's answers)

What the generator must draw, part by part. **Q** marks a choice for the owner.

**Cab** (one pressed steel cab on the frame, behind the engine)
- Two doors with drop glass and quarter vents; a one-piece windscreen curved round its
  corners [P F34]. A rear window in the back wall [D rear].
- Roof with three amber marker lamps over the screen [P F34]; a sun visor ridge.
- Mirrors on long tubular arms from the door's front edge [P F34, D front].
- Steps under the doors, the fuel tank on the left frame rail behind the cab step [P S, O;
  F34 shows the right side bare there] (first read as the right rail: corrected 2026-10-28).

**Bonnet and front**
- Bonnet: an alligator-style top hinged at the rear, louvres on its sides [P F34, D side];
  separate front wings with deep round arches, wing-mounted indicators.
- Grille panel: body colour with horizontal slots and the "ЗИЛ" badge (1962–76), or the
  **white** panel of the 130-76/80 [P F34w, F34r]. Answered in §6: the white panel.
- Two round head lamps in the panel's ends, round indicators under them, side lights.
- Bumper: a black steel channel with two tow hooks [P F34].

**Chassis**
- Ladder frame (two channels), front beam axle on leaf springs, rear axle on leaf springs.
- Wheels: single front, **dual rear**, 20" steel disc wheels [P S]; the spare under the
  platform's rear [P R34]: **not built** (owner, 2026-10-28: not needed; O shows none).
- Exhaust along the right rail.

**Platform** (open flatbed, owner 2026-10-28)
- Wooden floor on steel cross members, three wooden drop sides with steel uprights and
  latches; tail board drops; a front board over the cab's back [P R34].
- Answered in §6: steel, opening.
- Rear lamps and the plate lamp on the frame's end, mud flaps [P R34].
- Under the platform a continuous dark side beam along its whole length, a toolbox under
  its front behind the cab on the left [O].

**Interior**: a bench for three, a large thin-rimmed wheel, a simple dash with round dials,
a long floor lever. Answered in §6: full, plain.

**Opening parts**: doors, bonnet (engine access, owner 2026-10-28), drop sides if P2.

## 5. What is new for the generator

The car generator lofts one tub (`body.ts`); a bonneted truck is separate parts:

1. **Frame**: two rails and cross members at the frame height, the axles under it.
2. **Cab**: its own small loft (floor to roof) standing on the frame behind the engine,
   with the existing greenhouse code for its glass (columns, rail, screen).
3. **Bonnet and wings**: a narrow bonnet between separate wings, the grille panel at its
   front.
4. **Platform**: a box of boards on the frame behind the cab.
5. **Running gear**: dual rear wheels and a truck wheel set; and truck physics (mass,
   gearing, the vehicle model is car-only today): checked separately with the owner.

## 6. The owner's answers (2026-10-28)

- **F1**: the **white grille panel** of the late 130-76/80 [P F34w, F34r].
- **P1**: the platform **steel**, painted, as a trailer's or the GTA SA flatbed's: pressed
  steel drop sides with ribs, not boards.
- **P2**: the **tail board and the side boards open** (moving parts). Later: **ramps**, so a
  car can be driven up into the platform (not every car will fit: inside 3.75 × 2.33 m).
- **I1**: a **full cabin**, the player can drive it; a truck's is plain: a bench, a big
  thin wheel, a flat painted dash with a few round dials, a long floor lever.
